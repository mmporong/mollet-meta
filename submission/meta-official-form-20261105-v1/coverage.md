## Claims excluded

- No model-superiority, SAM tracking-accuracy, or generalization-accuracy claim.
- No invented time savings, cost savings, purchasing benefit, or performance improvement.
- Model output gains no verdict, approval, reward-change, or execution authority.
- No personal information, API key, or individual survey answer is published.

All robot footage and state are recorded simulation evidence, not physical-robot validation.
